# Acknowledgements and source terms

- **Middle-earth** was created by J. R. R. Tolkien. This is a fan project independent of the Tolkien Estate, MUME and the Arda GIS project.
- **MUME (Multi-Users in Middle-earth)** builders, maintainers and community created the playable world and game data. **MMapper** contributors built the client and documented the map format in its source. The bundled foreground is the exact MUME/arda tag 42 snapshot shipped with MMapper 26.06.0. The bundled-map repository describes its own distribution as fair use and grants no broader rights over the game data. [MUME](https://mume.org/) · [MMapper](https://github.com/MUME/MMapper) · [Bundled map notice](https://github.com/MUME/arda/blob/14d6bec24045394d72890f9bcc8c1098170e1941/README.md)
- **Faine** and **Nazgum** made and maintained the respective player maps. Both exact snapshots were obtained from Nazgum’s public MUME archive. The older Faine map and the August 2026 Nazgum map contain MUME-derived data. The archive’s GPL file does not establish a separate grant over the original MUME game data. [Pinned player maps](https://github.com/nazgum/mume/tree/b7ae4920fe43c7c883bf52c4e0b5d2eaa7e3c4d5/maps)
- **Pete Lewis** ([therealJiko](https://github.com/therealJiko)) created the Arda 32k heightmap, based on **ME-DEM** by **monks** and **Redrobes**. The creator released the 32k heightmap under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Here it was warped into MUME coordinates, resampled and used to render transparent hillshade. [32k source release](https://github.com/bburns/Arda/releases/tag/dem-32k-v1) · [Permission discussion](https://github.com/bburns/Arda/issues/4)
- **ME-GIS** contributors from the Outerra community assembled the original geographic layers. Thanks to **monks**, **Redrobes**, and **SeerBlue** for their original contributions, **jvangeld** and **Andrew Heiss** for maintaining the GIS, **tetrakai1** for the GeoPackage conversion and corrections, and **bburns** for curating Arda and publishing the 32k release. Arda records the older GIS vectors’ licence as uncertain. [ME-GIS](https://github.com/andrewheiss/ME-GIS) · [Arda history and terms](https://github.com/bburns/Arda/blob/295d735320affed232a0eadffeafb0482b660971/README.md#license)

The terrain-only backgrounds use MUME outdoor sectors and ME-GIS geography. The foreground inputs, backgrounds, comparison images and raw geographic data retain the terms of their respective sources. This repository credits those sources and pins the exact revision of each playable map. Keep this file with any authorised redistribution.
